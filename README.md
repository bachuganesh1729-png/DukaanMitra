🛒 DukaanMitra

Your Voice-Powered Grocery Ordering Assistant

DukaanMitra is a voice-enabled grocery ordering application designed to make digital grocery shopping easier for local customers and Kirana stores through natural language voice interactions.

💡 Problem Statement

Traditional grocery ordering in local Kirana stores often involves manual communication and order management. Language barriers and manual processes can make digital adoption difficult, especially for customers who prefer speaking in their regional language.

🚀 Our Solution

DukaanMitra enables customers to place grocery orders using their voice. It supports regional-language interactions, product quantity modification, order confirmation, and inventory visibility.

✨ Key Features

Voice-based grocery ordering.

Telugu speech recognition.

Product and quantity extraction from spoken requests.

Modify quantities before confirming orders.

Voice responses using text-to-speech.

Inventory dashboard with product availability and pricing.

Order management backed by SQLite.

🧠 Gnani Integration

DukaanMitra integrates Gnani speech technology:

Speech-to-Text (STT): Converts spoken grocery requests into text.

Text-to-Speech (TTS): Generates voice responses using the selected Suhana voice.

Regional Language Support: Demonstrated using Telugu voice input.

🔄 How It Works

Customer speaks a grocery order.

Gnani STT converts speech into text.

The application processes product names and quantities.

Customer can modify the order.

Customer confirms the order.

Gnani TTS provides voice feedback.

🎯 Objective

To make grocery ordering more accessible, convenient, and language-friendly for local customers and small grocery businesses.
